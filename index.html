Who I am
I'm Jashan — a builder, occasional overthinker, and incoming UWaterloo CE student who once glued a robot arm together with a hot glue gun at 2am.

From CNC machines and robot arms to data pipelines and AI tools — I operate across the full stack. I competed at OntarioSkills in robotics, co-led a 200+ member DECA chapter, won DECA Provincials, and somehow landed two internships before finishing high school.

I attended the Quantum School at UWaterloo — because regular physics clearly wasn't giving me enough existential dread. Now I'm building AI tools at Ascendance Foundry before heading to CE at Waterloo in September.

When I'm not building things: rewatching Iron Man trying to reverse-engineer the suit, playing Pokémon on a calculator during class, or staring at the night sky wondering why space is so much bigger than a GitHub repo.
